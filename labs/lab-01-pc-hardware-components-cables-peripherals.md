# Lab 01 - PC Hardware, Components, Cables, and Peripherals

## Objectives

- Identify common PC components.
- Compare RAM and storage types.
- Recognize cables and connectors.
- Match symptoms to hardware categories.

## Steps

1. Review a desktop or laptop hardware diagram.
2. Identify motherboard, CPU, RAM, storage, cooling, expansion slots, and power connectors.
3. Compare DIMM and SO-DIMM memory.
4. Compare HDD, SATA SSD, and NVMe SSD.
5. Identify USB-A, USB-C, Thunderbolt, HDMI, DisplayPort, VGA, Ethernet, SATA, and audio connectors.
6. Review printer types and common consumables.
7. Match symptoms to likely categories: no power, overheating, no display, no boot, grinding noise, and paper jam.
8. Create a hardware inventory table.
9. Add safety notes for component handling.

## Validation

- Components are identified correctly.
- Connector names and use cases are documented.
- Symptom table maps issues to likely areas.
- Safety notes include ESD and power precautions.

## Review Questions

1. What is the difference between SATA SSD and NVMe SSD?
2. Why is ESD protection important?
3. What symptoms suggest overheating?
4. Why should power supplies not be opened?
