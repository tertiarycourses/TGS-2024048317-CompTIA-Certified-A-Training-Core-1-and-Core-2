# Lab 02 - Mobile Devices, Networking, and Wireless

## Objectives

- Review mobile device features.
- Identify network settings.
- Match common ports and protocols.
- Compare wireless technologies.

## Steps

1. Review laptop parts such as display, battery, keyboard, touchpad, webcam, antennas, and DC jack.
2. Review mobile accessories such as USB-C, Lightning, docks, headsets, and Bluetooth devices.
3. View IP configuration on a PC.
4. Identify IPv4 address, subnet mask, default gateway, DNS server, and MAC address.
5. Review private IPv4 ranges and IPv6 link-local addresses.
6. Match common ports: 22, 25, 53, 67/68, 80, 110, 143, 443, 445, 3389.
7. Identify router, switch, access point, modem, firewall, hub, and patch panel roles.
8. Compare 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Wi-Fi.
9. Review Bluetooth pairing and mobile synchronization options.
10. Draw a simple home or office network diagram.

## Validation

- Network settings are identified correctly.
- Port table is complete.
- Wireless comparison includes range and interference.
- Network diagram shows device roles.

## Review Questions

1. What does DHCP provide to a client?
2. What does DNS do?
3. Why does 2.4 GHz usually travel farther than 5 GHz?
4. What is the role of a firewall?
